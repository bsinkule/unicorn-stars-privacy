Unicorn Stars: Privacy Policy and Support

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Unicorn Stars is a tapping and coloring game for young children on iPhone and iPad. This page explains what it does with information, and how to get help.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos, and it has no links that take a child out of the game.

What stays on the device

Your child's progress and the coloring pictures they save are stored only on your device, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes them. Saved pictures are not added to your photo library and are never sent anywhere.

Internet use

The game does not need an internet connection and does not send any data anywhere. If it is installed through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports and any feedback you choose to send, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

The game is made for children and collects no information from anyone, including children. It contains no third-party code that collects data.

Changes

If this changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
